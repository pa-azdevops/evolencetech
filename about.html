About us

A focused, founder-led agency for local businesses.

Evolence Tech is based in Nagpur, Maharashtra, and works with clinics and local businesses that want a straightforward system for getting found and getting booked — not a bloated retainer full of things they don't need.

Why we exist

Most local businesses are invisible where it matters.

A patient searching "dentist near me" or a homeowner searching "AC repair Nagpur" picks from whoever shows up first with the best reviews — usually within seconds. Most local businesses lose that moment simply because no one has ever properly set up their Google presence or followed up fast enough on the inquiries they do get.

How we're different

We stay small on purpose.

We work with a limited number of clients at a time so every account gets direct attention from the person actually doing the work — not passed between account managers. What we can't do yet, we're upfront about; what we do take on, we do properly.

What we believe.

Outcomes over activity

More calls and bookings matter more than post counts or follower growth. We report on what actually affects your business.

Plain language

No jargon-heavy reports. If we can't explain what we did and why in plain English, we haven't done our job.

Honesty about results

We won't promise guaranteed numbers no one can guarantee. We'll tell you what's realistic, and what we're doing about it.

Systems, not one-offs

A single good post doesn't fix visibility. We build repeatable systems — for search, for reviews, and for follow-up.

Where we work

Based in Nagpur, working across Maharashtra and beyond.

All our work is done remotely, which means location is never a limit — but we understand the Nagpur and Maharashtra local-business market especially well, and that's where we focus first.
